War Chronicle Browser Update 0535
=================================

Update 0535 (v0.5.0.35):
- Feuer Frei! now lets Take the Firepots use an occupied, undamaged Resource space when the Baggage Train is full.
- After choosing Take the Firepots with no empty space, choose which carried Resource space to purge; the full contents of that space are discarded and the Firepots are placed there.
- Take the Firepots remains unavailable only when no undamaged empty or purgeable Resource space exists.
- The Road Index now displays Preserve the Tablets correctly: Pass: Gain 1 Research and 1 Rapport. Fail: Lose 1 Coin.
- Retains all Update 0534 fixes and bundled Rulebook Draft 0.5.8.3.
- Bumped CSS/helper/data cache tokens and app version to 0535. SaveVersion remains 1.

Both WarChronicle.Web and WarChronicle.Pages are included.
